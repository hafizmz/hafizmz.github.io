🌏 UNESCO Biosphere Reserve · since 2021

A living rainforest, from hilltop to sea

On 15 September 2021, UNESCO named Penang Hill Malaysia's third Biosphere Reserve, after Tasik Chini and the Crocker Range. It protects an ancient rainforest while people continue to live, work and play around it.

Safety & about

⚠ Slippery when wet. Mossy paths and old concrete drains stay slick after rain ("Laluan licin ketika basah"). Wear shoes with grip.

Before you set off

  • No permit or fee is needed to hike. Trails are open year-round, but it gets hot and humid later in the day, so start early.
  • Carry water. There are no shops on the longer hikes.
  • Tell someone your route, and download an offline map (PHC recommends OsmAnd).
  • Don't feed the monkeys. Watch the trail edges for snakes; several local species are venomous.
  • Stay on marked paths. This is a protected biosphere reserve.

Emergency: 999 (112 from mobile) · Clinic at the Upper Station · AED at the Lower Station, and others shown in the official app
