Privacy

Penang Hill Trail Guide (beta). Last updated 10 November 2026.

This is an independent guide. It is not affiliated with Penang Hill Corporation (PHC) or the Penang Hill Biosphere Reserve.

Beta sign-up: what we collect

To use the beta you enter your email address once on each phone. We collect:

  • your email address
  • your name, if you choose to give it
  • the app language you were using, and the date and time you signed up
  • your browser and phone type (the "user agent"), so we can test on the phones testers actually use

Why: to know who is testing the beta, and to contact you about updates and feedback. We do not use it for anything else, and we never sell or share it.

How it is sent: the details go through FormSubmit, a form-to-email service, to the developer's email inbox. FormSubmit has its own privacy policy. If you sign up while offline, the details wait on your phone and are sent when you are back online.

How long we keep it: until the beta ends, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices: you can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by contacting the developer (below). If you tap "I've already joined", nothing is sent.

Location

If you tap Start GPS or turn on stop alerts, your phone asks for permission to share your location with the app. Your location is used only on your phone: to show where you are, alert you at stops, record a hike, or create a message you choose to share. It is never uploaded.

Stored on your phone

Your settings, visited stops, badges and sign-up details are saved on your phone so the app remembers them. GPX files you open or record stay on your phone. The app also saves its own pages, fonts and icons so it works without signal. Delete the app from your home screen, or clear site data in your browser, to remove all of this.

No ads or analytics

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

Outside links

The Library links to other websites (for example Wikipedia, Penang Hill Corporation and Wikiloc). Those sites have their own privacy policies.
