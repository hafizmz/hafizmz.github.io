Privacy

Penang Hill Trail Guide (beta). Last updated 9 November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. It does not send any of your data to us or to anyone else.

Location

If you tap Start GPS, your phone asks for permission to share your location with the app. Your location is used only on your phone: to show your position on a track, to record a hike, or to create a message you choose to share. It is never uploaded. You can turn GPS off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time.

Files

GPX files you open or record stay on your phone. Recorded tracks are saved to your phone's Downloads/Files.

Offline storage

The app saves its own pages, fonts and icons on your phone so it works without signal. You can remove them by deleting the app from your home screen or clearing site data in your browser.

Outside links

The Library links to other websites (for example Wikipedia, Penang Hill Corporation and Wikiloc). Those sites have their own privacy policies.
