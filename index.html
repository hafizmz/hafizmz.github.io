01 — ABOUT

BIO

Hafiz studied medical physics at Universiti Sains Malaysia, then completed his PhD in radiotherapy physics at the Joint Department of Physics of the Institute of Cancer Research and the Royal Marsden Hospital, London. There he contributed to developing prototype CMOS image sensors for real-time radiotherapy verification, within MI-3, an EPSRC-funded consortium spanning UCL and the Rutherford Appleton Laboratory.

In 2012 he returned to Malaysia to join USM, where he set up the quality assurance programme for its image-guided IMRT linac. He was Deputy Director (Academic & International) from 2019 to 2022, became Associate Professor in 2023, and has led the Department of Biomedical Imaging since 2024.

His main research interest is to make targeted radiotherapy more precise, using better image guidance and real-time tracking. Beyond the research he serves the profession nationally and internationally, and contributes to relevant outreach programmes.

02 — RESEARCH FOCUS

Radiotherapy imaging & instrumentation

Translating novel image sensors and radiation detectors into the radiotherapy clinic, from measuring dose in three dimensions to confirming, in real time, that the patient and the beam are where the plan expects.

Dosimetry

3D PRESAGE dosimeters read out with in-house optical CT for photon and proton beams, end-to-end dosimetry audit, and real-time treatment verification and patient-specific QA using linac log data.

Imaging

CMOS image sensors, time-of-flight surface imaging for breath-hold radiotherapy, and cone-beam CT image guidance and image-quality analysis.

PRESIDENT · MALAYSIAN ASSOCIATION OF MEDICAL PHYSICS

Leading MAMP with a strategic focus

As President, I lead MAMP's 2026 strategic roadmap, which sets out to move the association for sustainability from individuals' efforts to working systems. Its three priorities are stronger governance with a digitised, sustainable secretariat; closer engagement with stakeholders, from a national expertise database to a path towards professional certification; and a more united community across public, private, academic and East Malaysian medical physicists.
